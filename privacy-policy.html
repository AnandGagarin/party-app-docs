Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Thank you for using Movie Quiz (“the App”).

Your privacy is important to us. The App is a movie quiz game for Android and iOS, with question packs for Tamil and English cinema. You can play the daily challenge (FDFS · Daily Challenge), play alone in Solo Show and appear on a shared leaderboard, or play Party Mode, where two teams share one phone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Information stored on your device

  • Party Mode gameplay: team names, the optional “Tonight’s Cast” roster names, answers marked Got it / Missed, scores and round progress. This information stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Daily Challenge results: whether you solved or missed each day’s question, your solve time and your streak.
  • Profile and settings: your player name, your Solo Show personal best, last chosen game length, sound, haptics and daily reminder settings.

Information stored on our servers

  • Anonymous account ID: when you first open the App, it signs in anonymously and receives a random account ID. You do not provide a name, email address or phone number to get it. The ID is stored with your stage name and scores so the leaderboard can tell players apart.
  • Solo Show results: for each completed Solo Show game, your stage name (nickname), your overall score and your score for each round. Your nickname and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboard.
  • Invite codes: if you redeem an invite code in Settings, we store the code and the question pack it unlocks together with your account ID, so the App keeps showing that pack.

Technical information

  • When the App loads questions, the daily challenge and images, our hosting and content-delivery providers receive standard request data such as your IP address, device type, operating system, app version and the time of the request.

Install referrer (Android only)

The first time you open the App on Android, it reads the Google Play Install Referrer once. If you installed the App from a shared Daily Challenge link, this lets the App open that day’s challenge. The referrer is checked on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Beta signup

If you sign up for the beta on our website, we collect the email address you provide and use it only to send beta invitations and updates about the App. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

Account information: The App does not ask you to create an account or provide your email address or phone number to play.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information in team names, roster names or your stage name. Your stage name is shown publicly on the leaderboard.

How Information Is Used

  • To run the quiz, keep score and track game progress.
  • To show the Solo Show leaderboard, your rank and your personal best.
  • To run the Daily Challenge, including your streak and the optional reminder.
  • To remember your name, settings and unlocked question pack.
  • To deliver questions and images, keep the service working, and prevent abuse of the leaderboard.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

How Your Data Is Stored

Party Mode data, Daily Challenge results, your personal best and settings are stored only on your device. Your anonymous account ID, Solo Show scores, nickname and any redeemed invite code are stored on our servers, hosted by Supabase, so the leaderboard works. Images are stored with Cloudflare. These servers may be located outside your country.

Uninstalling the App removes data stored on your device. It does not remove your leaderboard entries from our servers. If you reinstall, the App creates a new anonymous account ID. See “Data Retention and Deletion” to request removal of server data.

Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information is processed by the following third parties only to operate the App:

  • Supabase: provides anonymous sign-in, stores leaderboard data, invite code redemptions and beta signup email addresses, and serves questions and the daily challenge. Supabase receives standard request data such as your IP address.
  • Cloudflare (R2 storage): serves images used in the App. Cloudflare receives standard request data such as your IP address.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store: process information under their own privacy policies when you download or update the App. On Android, Google Play also provides the install referrer described above.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Sharing from the App

When you tap “Share the night” or share a Daily Challenge result, the App creates an image or text and opens your device’s share sheet. You choose the app or person it goes to. We do not receive a copy. Shared Daily Challenge text may include a link to gagsylabs.com that opens the challenge in the App.

Analytics and Advertising

The App does not display advertisements and does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load questions, the daily challenge and images, and to update the leaderboard.
  • Notifications: asked for only if you turn on the daily challenge reminder in Settings (Android 13 and later, and iOS). The reminder is scheduled on your device; no push server is used.
  • Run at startup (Android): lets the App restore your scheduled reminder after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration (Android): for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Network state (Android): to check whether you are online before loading questions.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, such as a stage name on the leaderboard, contact us and we will remove it.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App, including encrypted connections between the App and our servers. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

  • On your device: you can change your name and settings in the App, or remove all local data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in your device settings.
  • On our servers: to delete your leaderboard entries, nickname, scores, invite code redemption and account ID, email support.party-app@gagsylabs.com with the subject “Delete my data”. Include your stage name and, if you can, the approximate dates and scores of your games, so we can find the right entries. We will delete the data within 30 days.
  • Beta signup: email us to have your email address removed.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent to its use. To make a request, email support.party-app@gagsylabs.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Your Choices

You choose what names to enter in the App. You can play Party Mode and the Daily Challenge without appearing on the leaderboard. You can turn off the daily reminder, sound and haptics in Settings, manage notification permission in your device settings, and request deletion of your data at any time.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be published with an updated “Last Updated” date. Your continued use of the App after an update means that you have had an opportunity to review the revised policy.
